## Ordering notes

A poll must exist before it can be listed or voted on, so **create a poll
(001)** comes first; **list (002)** and **vote (003)** both depend on it.

Feature 000 is fixed: no product feature starts before the quality pipeline is
installed and proven (type-check, lint, commit-msg, pre-commit staged-only, and
coverage gates rejecting automatically — skill `workflows/quality-pipeline`).
